2. How this is calculated

The new loan limit is property value multiplied by your target loan-to-value percentage. Estimated cash out is that limit minus the current loan balance and refinance closing costs. Lender limits, appraisal results, credit, taxes and fees can change the real figure substantially.

Questions

Is this an approval? No. It is arithmetic on your inputs. Lenders use their own underwriting and property rules.

Does this include taxes or cash needed at closing? No. Add any escrow, prepayment penalty or cash-to-close requirements yourself.

Is refinancing always worthwhile? Not necessarily. Compare the full cost of the new loan with your current loan and your goals.
