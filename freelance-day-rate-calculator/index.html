Questions

What is a realistic billable-days number? It varies by role. Count only days you can genuinely invoice after weekends, holidays, admin and time off.

Does this include tax? No. Set a higher target income or buffer if you need to cover tax.

Should I charge by day or by project? A day rate is one pricing method. Compare with project or value-based pricing for your market.
