2. How this is calculated

Self-employment tax is estimated as 12.4% Social Security on net earnings up to the annual wage base (reduced by other W-2 wages) plus 2.9% Medicare on all net earnings. Net earnings are 92.35% of net profit. The wage base changes each year, so confirm the current figure with the IRS.

Questions

Is this my total tax bill? No. This covers self-employment tax only. Federal and state income tax are separate and are not included.

What is the 92.35% figure? It is the standard factor used to convert net profit to net earnings subject to self-employment tax.

Can I deduct half of this? There is a standard deduction for half of self-employment tax on US returns. Confirm current rules with a tax professional or the IRS.
