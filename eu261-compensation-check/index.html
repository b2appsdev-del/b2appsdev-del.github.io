Questions

Is this a claims service? No. It is a free calculator plus information. It does not file anything, does not represent you, and is not legal advice.

What do the €250 / €400 / €600 bands mean? EU261 sets indicative compensation bands by flight distance and delay at arrival. The real amount depends on your exact route, carrier, and whether exceptions apply. Check the regulation and official guidance for your flight.

Do I have to use a claims company? No. You can submit a claim directly to the airline yourself, free of charge. A claims company is optional and usually charges a success fee. Compare the fee before you decide.

Do you get a commission from claims companies? Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you use them and a claim succeeds, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This is disclosed before every such link and does not change the amount you receive.

Does this guarantee compensation? No. There is no guarantee of eligibility, a payout, an amount, or any outcome. Rules and airline policies change; verify current rules from official sources.
