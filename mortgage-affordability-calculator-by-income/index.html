2. How this is calculated

The estimate multiplies gross monthly income by your chosen debt-to-income percentage, subtracts other monthly debt payments, then converts the remaining housing payment into a loan amount using the standard fixed-rate amortization formula. It is a rough illustration, not an approval or an offer of credit.

Questions

Is this a mortgage approval? No. It is a rough arithmetic illustration. Lenders use their own underwriting, credit, income verification and property rules.

What DTI should I use? Different lenders and loan programs use different limits. Check current official program guidelines rather than assuming a number.

Does this include taxes and insurance? No. Property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA dues and mortgage insurance can materially change what you can afford.
