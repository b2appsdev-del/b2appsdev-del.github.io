Emergency Fund Calculator by Monthly Expenses

Set an emergency-fund target from essential monthly expenses and see roughly what you would need to set aside each month. Runs locally.

1. Calculate

Enter your own figures and press the button. Nothing leaves your device.

2. How this is calculated

The target is essential monthly expenses multiplied by the number of months you want to cover. The monthly saving figure is the remaining gap divided by your chosen number of months. It assumes no interest and no changes in expenses or income.

Questions

How many months should I target? It depends on your job stability, dependents and other resources. Six months is a common illustration, not a rule.

Should I count all expenses? Use essential expenses you would still need if income stopped, not discretionary spending.

Is this financial advice? No. It is simple arithmetic on your inputs. Consider your own circumstances and get professional advice where needed.
