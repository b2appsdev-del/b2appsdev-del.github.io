2. How this is calculated

This rough estimate combines self-employment tax (12.4% Social Security up to the entered wage base, reduced by W-2 wages, plus 2.9% Medicare on 92.35% of net profit) with a simple flat-rate income-tax estimate, subtracts tax already paid, then divides by four. It ignores deductions, credits, QBI, brackets, withholding, state-specific rules and safe-harbor calculations.
