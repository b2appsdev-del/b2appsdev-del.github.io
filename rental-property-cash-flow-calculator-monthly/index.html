2. How this is calculated

Effective rent is monthly rent reduced by your vacancy allowance. Monthly cash flow is effective rent minus operating expenses, mortgage payment and maintenance reserve. Taxes, management fees and one-off repairs are not modeled unless you include them in your expense input.
